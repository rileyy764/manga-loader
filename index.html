Manga Subscriptions Loader

1

Install bookmarklet

Copy the code below and create a new bookmark in your browser with it as the URL.

javascript:(async function(){let clickCount=0;while(true){let viewMoreBtn=document.querySelector('button[hx-get][hx-swap]');if(viewMoreBtn&&viewMoreBtn.textContent.includes('View More')){viewMoreBtn.click();clickCount++;console.log(`Clicked "View More" #${clickCount}...`);await new Promise(resolve=>setTimeout(resolve,1000))}else{console.log('All subscriptions loaded!');break}}let titles=Array.from(document.querySelectorAll('article.bg-base-300 h2')).map(el=>el.textContent.trim()).filter(title=>title.length>0);console.log(`Found ${titles.length} manga!`);console.log(titles.join('\n'));navigator.clipboard.writeText(titles.join('\n')).then(()=>{alert('✓ Copied '+titles.length+' titles to clipboard!')})})();
2

Go to your subscriptions

Head to weebcentral.com/users/me/subscriptions

3

Click the bookmarklet

Click your newly created bookmark from the bookmarks bar. It will load all your subscriptions and copy the titles to your clipboard.

That's it

Your manga titles are ready to paste anywhere you need them.
